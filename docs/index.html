1. Overview

Skrew Calculator (سكرو) ("the App", "we", "our", or "us") is a companion tool for calculating and tracking round scores for the Skrew card game. We aim to describe the App's data practices clearly.

This policy explains what information may be collected, how it may be used, and the choices available to you. It describes the App's current practices and is not legal advice.

2. Information Handled Directly by the App

2.1 No Account or Personal Identity Information

The App does not require registration, login, or personal profile creation. We do not solicit or record your name, physical address, email address, phone number, or payment details.

2.2 Local Storage Only (On-Device)

  • Game Tallies & Player Names: Custom player names and score records entered during gameplay are intended to remain on your device using the App's local storage.
  • Preferences: User settings, such as your selected language (Arabic or English), are stored strictly on-device.
  • No Developer Cloud Sync: The developer does not operate a gameplay database or account-sync service. Local records can be erased by clearing application storage or uninstalling the App. Advertising providers may still process separate technical data as described below.

3. Third-Party Advertising (Google AdMob)

To maintain Skrew Calculator as a free tool, we integrate Google AdMob (operated by Google LLC) to serve advertisements (banners and interstitial ads).

3.1 Information Collected Automatically by Google AdMob

When you run the App, Google AdMob and its advertising partners may automatically collect certain diagnostic and technical information, including:

  • Advertising Identifiers: Mobile advertising identifiers such as the Google Advertising ID (GAID / AAID) on Android, or the Identifier for Advertisers (IDFA) on iOS (if tracking authorization is granted), as well as vendor identifiers (IDFV).
  • Approximate Location: Your Internet Protocol (IP) address is used by Google to estimate coarse (city- or country-level) location for localized ads and ad security.
  • Device Technical Information: Hardware model, device manufacturer, operating system version, screen resolution, network connection type, and mobile carrier.
  • Performance & Ad Interaction Telemetry: Ad impressions, user click activity, ad load latency, and anonymized diagnostic crash logs.

Play Console disclosure: Google Play's Data safety declaration is maintained separately and must match the advertising SDKs and consent settings used by the released build. Review both disclosures whenever the build or SDK configuration changes.

3.2 Purposes of Processing

Google processes this data to:

  • Serve relevant ads (contextual or personalized, based on your consent preferences).
  • Prevent duplicate ads through frequency capping.
  • Detect and prevent click fraud, invalid traffic, and security threats.
  • Compile aggregated advertising performance metrics.
Important Link: Learn how Google handles information when you use partner apps by reading How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services and the Google Privacy Policy.

4. Cookies, Device Storage & Google EU User Consent

Where applicable, the App uses Google's consent tools to support the Google EU User Consent Policy and relevant privacy requirements:

  • Storage of Identifiers: Google and its authorized advertising partners may store and access cookies, mobile identifiers, and device storage to deliver ads, measure ad effectiveness, and combat abuse.
  • Consent Framework: Where the App presents a User Messaging Platform (UMP) consent dialogue, it gives you choices regarding identifiers and personalized advertising.
  • Ad Technology Providers: Where provided by the consent management dialogue, Google discloses the ad technology providers used for the relevant request.

5. European User Rights (GDPR & UK GDPR)

If you are situated in the EEA, the UK, or Switzerland, you possess specific legal rights regarding your personal data:

  • Right to Withdraw Consent: Where consent is the legal basis, you can withdraw or adjust your consent choices at any time.
  • Right of Access & Erasure: You may have the right to request access to or deletion of personal data, subject to applicable law and the relevant provider's role.
  • Right to Object: You can object to data processing based on legitimate interests for advertising.
  • Right to Lodge a Complaint: You can submit a grievance to your national Data Protection Authority (DPA).

How to Manage or Withdraw Your Consent:

  1. In-App Privacy Settings: Access the consent options dialogue inside the App to reconfigure your ad preferences.
  2. Android Device Settings: Open Settings > Google > Ads and select Delete advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads.
  3. iOS Device Settings: Open Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking and disable tracking permissions for Skrew Calculator.

6. California & US State Privacy Disclosures (CCPA / CPRA)

Under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) and California Privacy Rights Act (CPRA):

  • No Monetary Sale: We do not sell your personal information for monetary payment.
  • Cross-Context Behavioral Advertising: Third-party ad networks may utilize device identifiers to provide interest-based advertising. You may opt out of this sharing via your device's operating system advertising controls as outlined above.
  • Non-Discrimination: We will not discriminate against you for exercising applicable privacy rights.

7. Children's Online Privacy (COPPA & GDPR-K)

Skrew Calculator is designed for general audiences and card game enthusiasts. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 years of age (or under 16 in the European Union). If we become aware that a child has provided personal information without verified parental consent, we will promptly take steps to delete that data. If you have concerns, please reach out to us at kirolos.esmat10@gmail.com.

8. Data Retention & Security

  • Local Data: All local match tallies and language preferences remain on your device until you uninstall the App or clear app storage.
  • Advertising Logs: Data gathered by Google AdMob is retained in accordance with Google's retention policies.
  • Encryption: Transmission of ad requests occurs securely over encrypted Transport Layer Security (TLS/HTTPS).
