## 1. Overview

**Scrow Calculator (سكرو)** ("the App", "we", "our", or "us") is a score-tracking and calculator utility application designed for the Scrow card game. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting any information associated with your use of the App.

This Privacy Policy explains what information is collected, how it is used, and your rights regarding that information. This policy is formulated to comply with applicable data protection laws, including the **General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR)** (EU/EEA & UK), the **California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA / CPRA)**, and the **[Google EU User Consent Policy](https://www.google.com/about/company/user-consent-policy.html)**.

## 2. Information We Collect Directly

### 2.1 No Account or Personal Identity Information Required
The App does not require user registration, account creation, or login. We do not collect or request personally identifiable information (PII) such as your real name, email address, phone number, physical address, or financial payment details.

### 2.2 Local Device Storage Only
- **Game Records & History:** Scores, round tallies, and custom player nicknames that you enter during a game are saved strictly **on your local device** using local storage (`SharedPreferences`).
- **App Settings:** User interface preferences, including language selection (Arabic or English), are stored locally on your device.
- **No External Sync:** This local data is never sent, synchronized, or transmitted to any developer-owned or private external server. It resides entirely on your device and can be erased by clearing the App's data in system settings or uninstalling the App.

### 3.2 Purposes of Processing
Google AdMob uses this information for:

## 5. European Regulations (GDPR / UK GDPR) User Rights

If you reside in the European Economic Area (EEA), United Kingdom, or Switzerland, you have the following statutory rights under the GDPR:

- **Right to Withdraw Consent:** You have the right to withdraw or modify your consent choices at any time.
- **Right of Access:** You have the right to request information about the personal data processed about you.
- **Right to Erasure ("Right to Be Forgotten"):** You have the right to request deletion of your personal data.
- **Right to Restrict or Object to Processing:** You may object to or restrict processing of your data for advertising purposes.
- **Right to Lodge a Complaint:** You have the right to lodge a complaint with your local Data Protection Supervisory Authority (DPA).

### How to Withdraw or Change Consent:
1. **Via In-App Consent Form:** Whenever the UMP consent options form is triggered or accessible from the App settings, you can update your preferences.
2. **Via Device Settings:** You can reset or delete your advertising identifier at any time:
   - **Android:** Go to *Settings* > *Google* > *Ads* > *Delete advertising ID* or *Opt out of Ads Personalization*.
   - **iOS:** Go to *Settings* > *Privacy & Security* > *Tracking* > Disable *Allow Apps to Request to Track*.
