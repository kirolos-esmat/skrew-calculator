## 1. Overview

**Skrew Calculator (سكرو)** ("the App", "we", "our", or "us") is a score-tracking and calculator utility application designed for the Skrew card game. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting any information associated with your use of the App.

This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected, how it may be used, and the choices available to you. It is intended to describe the App's current data practices and to support applicable privacy and advertising requirements. It is not legal advice.

---

## 2. Information We Collect Directly

### 2.1 No Account or Personal Identity Information Required
The App does not require user registration, account creation, or login. We do not collect or request personally identifiable information (PII) such as your real name, email address, phone number, physical address, or financial payment details.

### 2.2 Local Device Storage Only
- **Game Records & History:** Scores, round tallies, and custom player nicknames that you enter during a game are intended to remain **on your local device** using the App's local storage.
- **App Settings:** User interface preferences, including language selection (Arabic or English), are stored locally on your device.
- **No Developer Cloud Sync:** The developer does not operate a gameplay database or account-sync service. Local records can be erased by clearing the App's data in system settings or uninstalling the App. Advertising providers may still process separate technical data as described below.

## 3. Third-Party Services and Advertising (Google AdMob)

For users who report an age of 18 or older, the App may display advertisements provided by **Google AdMob** (a service of Google LLC) after the applicable consent flow. The App asks for age before initializing the advertising SDK. Users who report an age under 18 can use the App without AdMob ads. The entered age is used for this decision during the current session and is not saved.

Google AdMob and its advertising partners collect and process certain technical data to serve advertisements, prevent fraud, and measure ad performance.

The Google Play **Data safety** declaration is maintained separately in Play Console and must be kept consistent with the advertising SDKs and consent settings used by the released build. If the released build or its SDK configuration changes, review both this policy and the Play Console declaration before publishing an update.

### 3.1 Data Collected by Google AdMob
When ads are enabled for an adult user, Google AdMob may collect:
- **Device and Advertising Identifiers:** Such as the Google Advertising ID (GAID / AAID) on Android, or the Identifier for Advertisers (IDFA) on iOS (if tracking permission is granted), and the Identifier for Vendor (IDFV).
- **IP Address:** Used to estimate coarse (approximate) location (e.g., country, city, or region) for regional advertising and fraud detection.
- **Device Information:** Device model, manufacturer, operating system version, screen resolution, mobile carrier, and system performance metrics.
- **Ad Interaction Data:** Information regarding ad impressions, clicks, views, and diagnostic load errors.

### 3.2 Purposes of Processing
Google AdMob uses this information for:
- Serving contextual or personalized advertisements (subject to your consent).
- Frequency capping (ensuring you do not see the same ad repeatedly).
- Aggregated reporting, billing, and performance analytics.
- Combating invalid traffic, click fraud, and malicious activity.

### 3.3 Google Privacy Policies

## 5. European Regulations (GDPR / UK GDPR) User Rights

If you reside in the European Economic Area (EEA), United Kingdom, or Switzerland, you have the following statutory rights under the GDPR:

- **Right to Withdraw Consent:** Where consent is the legal basis, you can withdraw or modify your consent choices at any time.
- **Right of Access:** You have the right to request information about the personal data processed about you.
- **Right to Erasure ("Right to Be Forgotten"):** You may have the right to request deletion of personal data, subject to applicable law and the relevant provider's role.
- **Right to Restrict or Object to Processing:** You may object to or restrict processing of your data for advertising purposes.
- **Right to Lodge a Complaint:** You have the right to lodge a complaint with your local Data Protection Supervisory Authority (DPA).

### How to Withdraw or Change Consent:
1. **Via In-App Consent Form:** Where Google requires a privacy options form, use the privacy icon in the App to update your advertising choices.
2. **Via Device Settings:** You can reset or delete your advertising identifier at any time:
   - **Android:** Go to *Settings* > *Google* > *Ads* > *Delete advertising ID* or *Opt out of Ads Personalization*.
   - **iOS:** Go to *Settings* > *Privacy & Security* > *Tracking* > Disable *Allow Apps to Request to Track*.
